Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This page explains how The Physics Beyond currently handles information when you visit this website.

Last updated: September 30, 2026

1. About this website

The Physics Beyond is a science and physics communication platform covering topics including everyday physics, quantum physics, relativity, cosmology, space, experiments and thought experiments.

This Privacy Policy applies to information associated with your use of The Physics Beyond website.

2. Information we collect

The current version of this website is primarily a static informational website. We do not intentionally request or collect personal information simply because you visit and read the site's pages.

The website does not currently provide a user-registration system or account system.

3. Contact information

If you choose to contact The Physics Beyond through the email address provided on the Contact page, the information you include in your email may be received and used to respond to your enquiry.

Email communications are handled through the relevant email service provider. Information contained in those communications may therefore be subject to that provider's own privacy practices and policies.

4. External links

The website may contain links to external websites and platforms, including YouTube and other third-party resources.

Once you leave The Physics Beyond and visit an external service, that service's own privacy policy and terms may apply. We recommend reviewing the privacy practices of external platforms before providing them with personal information.

5. Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently display Google AdSense advertisements. It uses AdSense code to verify the site for Google's review. The code may connect a visitor's browser to Google. The site does not intentionally use first-party cookies for user accounts, personalization or advertising.

We plan to apply to use Google AdSense. If the site is approved and advertising is enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to serve and measure ads. Depending on user and account settings, these technologies may also be used to personalize advertising. Google's explanation of how it uses information on partner sites is available at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Users can manage Google's personalized advertising settings at Google Ads Settings. Before any ads are served, this policy and any applicable consent settings will be reviewed to match the advertising features enabled on the site.

6. Third-party services

Some pages may link to or eventually incorporate services operated by third parties. Those services operate independently from The Physics Beyond and may collect information according to their own policies.

7. Children's privacy

The Physics Beyond is intended as a general science and educational resource. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through this website.

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features, services or data practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of The Physics Beyond, you can contact us at:

thephysicsbeyond@gmail.com
