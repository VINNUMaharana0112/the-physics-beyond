Website publishing

Sign in to the publishing studio to add or update articles, YouTube videos, Instagram Reels, physical books, digital products, and affiliate recommendations.

Manage website content

In the studio, choose a content type, add its details and images, then select Publish to make it visible on the website. Drafts stay private to your signed-in account.

Open the publishing studio

If this button opens project settings instead of the editor, select The Physics Beyond and open its Studio from your project dashboard.

What can be published now?

  • Articles with formatted text, headings, links, and uploaded images.
  • YouTube and Instagram links with captions and thumbnails.
  • Book and digital product listings with confirmed details and optional external checkout links.
  • Affiliate recommendations with a visible commission disclosure.

Payment processing and private digital delivery are separate. You can publish free resources, enquiry details, and products hosted at an external checkout now. Do not upload paid files as public downloads.
