Orders and fulfilment

Payment state is provider-confirmed. Physical stock is reserved when checkout begins and restored if payment fails. Shipping status can only advance on paid orders.

Affiliate recommendations

Affiliate offers are maintained separately from paid products and never create a Razorpay order. A clear commission disclosure is required before an offer can be active.
