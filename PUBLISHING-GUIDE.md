# Publish content on The Physics Beyond

Use the Publishing Studio at `/admin/` to add website content. The site reads published entries from Sanity; unpublished drafts do not appear to visitors. Publishing articles, video links, book listings, digital product details, and affiliate links does not depend on the payment integration.

## What to publish

- **Article:** add a title, a short introduction, formatted text, topic, and optional images. Use the link tool for references and sources.
- **YouTube video / Instagram Reel:** paste the public post URL, add a short caption, and upload an optional thumbnail.
- **Physical book:** add the cover, author, publisher, format, price only when confirmed, availability, shipping, and delivery estimate. If no checkout is ready, visitors can enquire by email.
- **Digital product:** add its format, what is included, price, and delivery details. A public download is only for a file that is meant to be freely shared. For paid files, use an external checkout/delivery service or wait for the private delivery setup; never upload paid files as public downloads.
- **Affiliate recommendation:** paste the complete affiliate URL and keep the disclosure text. The site marks the outbound link as sponsored.

## Publishing steps

1. Open `/admin/` and sign in to the Publishing Studio.
2. Choose the content type from the left menu and select **Create new**.
3. Fill in the fields and upload images in the Studio. Sanity generates a draft page address from the title; adjust it only if needed.
4. Select **Publish**. The content appears on the matching Articles, Videos, or Bookshelf section after the public content cache updates.
5. Open the public page and review the title, copy, media, price, and links.

The existing static articles, Higher Mathematics listing, free study planner, and affiliate recommendations remain as fallbacks until a published Studio item is added to their section. Once the section has published Studio entries, that section uses the Studio entries as its current list.
